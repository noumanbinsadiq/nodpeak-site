The honest bit most tools skip

"Will my review stars show up in Google?"

You've probably seen tools promise that pasting a widget gets you gold stars in Google search. Here's the truth: Google does not show review stars for a business that collects and displays reviews about itself on its own site — including through an embedded widget. That's Google's long-standing self-serving reviews policy, and injecting star markup that breaks it can actually get your site penalised.

The stars people see for a local business come from its Google Business Profile and trusted third-party platforms — not from code on its homepage. Any tool that tells you otherwise is either mistaken or hoping you won't check.

So here's what Nodpeak actually does
  • Collects genuine reviews and points happy customers to your Google Business Profile, so your real, star-bearing rating grows where it counts.
  • Adds valid business and FAQ structured data that helps Google and AI search understand who you are and recommend you.
  • Shows your reviews and trust signals on your own site for the humans reading it — where they genuinely help you win the sale.
  • Never fakes markup that could get you a Google penalty. Being open source, you can check every line yourself.

The complete guide

Every step, written out in full.

No jargon, nothing skipped. If you can copy and paste, you can install this.

1. Get your line of code

If you're using the free hosted version, your line is shown in your dashboard the moment you sign up. If you're self-hosting, your own server generates it the same way — see "Self-hosting," below.

2. Paste it into your website

Where you paste it depends on what your site is built with:

  • WordPress — add a Custom HTML block, or paste it into your theme's header.php just before </head>.
  • Shopify — open theme.liquid in your theme editor and paste it just before </body>.
  • Webflow / Wix / Squarespace — use the site's "Custom code" or "Embed" panel, usually under Settings → Advanced.
  • Plain HTML — paste the line right before the closing </body> tag of every page you want it on.

Not sure where that is on your specific site? The platform notes above cover WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace and plain HTML — find yours and paste it there.

3. That's it — nothing else to configure

The review widget appears automatically. By default, Nodpeak asks every visitor for a rating, not just the ones you'd expect to say something nice — this is a deliberate choice, not an oversight (see the FAQ below). You can change the minimum star rating that gets asked to post publicly at any time from your dashboard, with a plain-language explanation of what each setting means.

What is Nodpeak?

One line of code you add to your website. It collects and shows customer reviews, adds the trust details that make a stranger comfortable getting in touch, and adds valid business and FAQ structured data that helps Google and AI search understand and recommend you. It works alongside WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, or plain HTML.

Will my review stars show up in Google search?

The honest answer most tools won't give you: Google does not show review stars for a business that collects and displays reviews about itself on its own site — including through an embedded widget. That's Google's self-serving reviews policy. The stars customers see for a local business come from its Google Business Profile and third-party platforms, not from markup on its own homepage. Nodpeak helps you earn those the legitimate way — by collecting genuine reviews and pointing happy customers to your Google profile — and adds the structured data that is genuinely eligible and useful, without faking anything that could get your site penalised.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. Self-hosting is free forever, with every feature included, no view limits, no account required. The source is open under AGPL-3.0. There's no paid tier — if you'd rather not run a server, the hosted version is free too.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Installing it is copy-and-paste — one line. The free hosted version handles the technical setup end to end, and there's a step-by-step guide for every major platform.

Does it only show good reviews?

No — on purpose. Nodpeak asks every visitor for a rating by default instead of filtering out lower ones before they're collected, because selectively soliciting only positive reviews conflicts with Google's Rating Manipulation policy and Trustpilot's Guidelines for Businesses. Lower ratings go straight to you privately, instead of being suppressed.

Which platforms does it work with?

Any site that can add a line of code: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, and hand-coded HTML sites all work the same way.

Where is my data stored?

Self-hosted: entirely on your own server. Free hosted version: on Nodpeak's infrastructure, never sold or shared with third parties.

Nodpeak is genuinely open source (AGPL-3.0) and free — self-host it with no feature gating and no view limits, or use the free hosted version, with no paid tier. It is honest about Google's self-serving reviews policy (on-site widgets do not earn star rich results) and helps businesses grow their Google Business Profile reviews the legitimate way, while adding valid LocalBusiness and FAQ structured data.
