What can be built around my business?

Almost everything a customer sees, and everything that happens after they press send:

  • Your brand — your logo, colours and web address, so it looks like a native part of your site, not a bolt-on.
  • Your data — your real products, services and prices, and your own labour rates for instant quotes.
  • Your website — installed and integrated for you, on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace or plain HTML.
  • Your workflow — where new reviews, leads and enquiries go, connected to the quoting, booking, CRM or inbox tools you already use.
  • The systems you wish existed — custom automations built around the way your business runs, beyond what any off-the-shelf tool does.

Who will I be talking to?

A real person, from your first message to launch day. Every email, WhatsApp message and text is read and answered personally. There are no bots, no auto-replies, no call centre and no sales script — just a real conversation about your business and what would actually help it.

What does it cost?

It's custom, so the price depends on what you want built. You get a written scope and a clear number after a short conversation, and you pay nothing until you've approved both — and seen a preview with your own branding.

The tool itself stays free. This is only for people who'd rather have it built and integrated for them, and who want the custom systems around it.

Will the free Nodpeak stay free?

Yes — always. Self-hosting is free forever, and the hosted version is free and self-serve. This page is an optional add-on for businesses who want it done for them, not a catch and not a paywall on anything you already have.

Who owns the system and the data?

You do. It's your brand, your customers and your data — kept yours, and exportable on request. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
